Just found some of my old drawings, which might explain my capability for graphic designs
Written by: Yuk

Lore Intro

So recently I went home for Lunar New Year, and I found some of my old “drawings” lol, just thought I’d share them here. “Drawings” in quotations because, I guess some of these is closer to what artists call “trace”, yeah most of these is me looking at a reference and basically copying them, but there is some stuff I draw on my own, and I still think they tell a story or something at least. Back then, I used the alias “Zax” or “Zaclord” (son 😭), these probably dates way back to 2016, 10 frickin years lol.

After showing my drawings, I think might as well showcase my more recent “artistic” works in graphics design.

Paper drawings

Most of them are pencil, some are colored by those cheap colored pencils, but i especially like that one shaded work that was done using a ballpoint pen.

Let’s go deeper into their lore one by one, from left to right, up to down:

  1. Carol, a character from a gacha-rythm game called TapSonic TOP. This game was introduced to me by an old friend, I really like it and was quite good at it, unfortunately the game reached EOS :(.
  2. Alice, playable hero from a mobile MOBA Arena of Valor (AOV). I think this one wasn’t copied.
  3. If I’m not mistaken, Ivy from Cytus II, it seems I was already a big rhythm game guy lol, surprised I didn’t draw Osu’s mascot.
  4. Original work I think…
  5. Nekomiya Hinata, Vtuber, I don’t know how I knew about her back then lol, this pose is original I think.

Graphics design

I think I stopped drawing around 2020 or something, sometimes I did produce a few original works, but these days, I mostly do graphics design (which I dropped again for many reasons). Nevertheless, I’m going to showcase some of my works. I started this graphics design thing after I enrolled in HUS VNU, and join some clubs and stuff. I did these using Adobe products like Illustrator and Photoshop.
