Yuk (Yukano)

I’m currently studying Computer Science at Hanoi University of Science (HUS - VNU) as part of Vietnam National University. I’m in the second semester of my second year, and my main focus is machine learning and AI. These fields really interest me, and I enjoy exploring how they intersect with different areas, which is why I keep this blog to share thoughts and ideas related to them.

I try to live a balanced life, but it’s not always easy. I tend to spend a lot of time studying or working on projects, and while I know it’s important to take breaks, I often find myself immersed in what I’m doing. I’m always looking for ways to grow and improve, but sometimes that means I don’t always manage my time as well as I should.

I believe in treating people with respect and kindness, and I try to stay grounded despite the busyness of life. I think it’s important to connect with others on a human level, without labels or expectations. I’m always open to having conversations or getting to know people better, so feel free to reach out if you’d like to connect.
